    How do I find my review link?
    1. Easiest: sign in to the Google account that manages your business, search Google for your business name, and click Ask for reviews (or Get more reviews) on your Business Profile. Copy the link — it looks like https://g.page/r/…/review.
    2. Alternative: open Google's Place ID Finder, search for your business and copy its Place ID (starts with ChIJ). Paste it above and the review link is built for you.

    Always press Test link or scan the preview with your phone before printing.

    Print sheet

    • In the print dialog, set Scale to 100% (“Actual size”) so the stickers come out the size you chose.
    • Print on full-sheet sticker/label paper and cut along the dashed lines, or pick Save as PDF to send the file to a print shop.
    • For a glossy, waterproof finish on tables, laminate the sheet or use vinyl sticker paper.
